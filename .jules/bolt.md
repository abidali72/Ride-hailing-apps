## 2026-09-08 - O(1) Map Indexing for InMemoryDB Entity Lookups
**Learning:** `InMemoryDB` in `server/db/schema.js` previously performed linear array scans ($O(N)$) for every `findUser`, `findDriver`, `findTrip`, `findTrips`, and `getEarnings` call. In hotspot endpoints like driver matching (`findNearbyDrivers`) and trip listing/enrichment (`GET /api/trips`), performing repeated linear scans across users and drivers resulted in severe $O(N \cdot M)$ computational bottlenecks (~54s for 1000 driver match operations on modest dataset sizes).
**Action:** Use incremental Map indices (`Map<id, entity>`, `Map<foreign_key, entity[]>`) in `InMemoryDB` to provide $O(1)$ fast-path lookups while falling back to array scans for multi-key queries. Ensure incremental index updates on writes rather than full index rebuilds to keep insert/update costs at $O(1)$.
